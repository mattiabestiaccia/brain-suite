# Users

> Template for `.brainstorm/dimensions/users.md`
> Explorer uses these sections as conversation anchors during exploration.

## Primary User Persona

Who is the primary user? Be specific: demographics, psychographics, daily life, technical sophistication. Describe a real person, not a marketing segment. What do they care about? What frustrates them?

## User Jobs To Be Done

Functional jobs (what they are trying to accomplish), emotional jobs (how they want to feel), social jobs (how they want to be perceived). Use the JTBD format: "When I [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [outcome]."

## Current Alternatives

How they solve the problem today. What tools, workarounds, or manual processes do they use? What is painful about the current approach? What is good enough about it that they have not switched?

## User Journey

The path from discovery to advocacy: How do they find out about this? What makes them try it? What does first use look like? What makes them come back? What makes them tell someone else?

## Validation Plan

How to test assumptions about users before building. Specific experiments: who to talk to, what to ask, what signals to look for. Minimum evidence needed to proceed vs pivot.
