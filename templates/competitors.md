# Competitors

> Template for `.brainstorm/dimensions/competitors.md`
> Explorer uses these sections as conversation anchors during exploration.

## Direct Competitors

Who competes directly — same problem, same audience. For each: what they do, their strengths (be honest), their weaknesses, and their market position.

## Indirect Competitors & Substitutes

Alternative solutions users currently use to solve the same problem. Include manual workarounds, spreadsheets, "do nothing" as a competitor. What are people hiring today to do this job?

## Competitive Advantages

Your unfair advantage — what you can do that competitors cannot easily replicate. Consider: proprietary data, network effects, unique expertise, distribution advantage, cost structure advantage. Be brutally honest about defensibility.

## Competitive Risks

Who could copy you and how fast. What happens if a well-funded incumbent enters your space. What if a competitor pivots toward your niche? Worst-case competitive scenarios.

## Positioning

How you are different in one sentence. Not what you do — how you are perceived relative to alternatives. The positioning statement should make it obvious who this is for and who it is not for.
