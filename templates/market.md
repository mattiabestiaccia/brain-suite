# Market

> Template for `.brainstorm/dimensions/market.md`
> Explorer uses these sections as conversation anchors during exploration.

## Target Market

Who is the target market? Define the segment specifically — demographics, behaviors, size. How big is it and is it growing? Use bottom-up estimation, not top-down TAM fantasies.

## Market Dynamics

Trends working in your favor or against you. Regulatory environment. Timing — why now and not 2 years ago or 2 years from now?

## Go-to-Market Strategy

How you reach your first users. Channels, positioning, initial traction plan. Be specific about the first 100 users — where they are and how you get their attention.

## Pricing Strategy

Pricing model (subscription, usage-based, freemium, one-time). Price anchoring — what do users pay for comparable tools? Evidence of willingness to pay.

## Market Risks

Saturation risk, timing risk, regulatory risk, platform dependency risk. What external factors could make this market unattractive regardless of product quality?
