# Business

> Template for `.brainstorm/dimensions/business.md`
> Explorer uses these sections as conversation anchors during exploration.

## Revenue Model

How money flows in. Pricing tiers, what users pay for, unit economics. Be specific about what the transaction looks like — who pays, how much, how often.

## Cost Structure

Fixed costs vs variable costs. Major cost drivers and how they scale with users. Infrastructure, team, third-party services — what are the big line items?

## Key Metrics

North star metric — the one number that tells you this is working. Leading indicators that predict the north star. How you measure them and target values.

## Growth Levers

What drives adoption — virality, content, sales, partnerships, word of mouth? Which lever is primary and which are supporting? What makes growth sustainable vs one-time?

## Business Risks

Cash flow timing, dependency on key partners or platforms, single points of failure, regulatory exposure. What could break the business even if the product is good?
